The real study: finding GDMT gaps, accuracy against two clinicians' review

PreliminaryACC.27 abstract · 1,000 heart-failure patients · 2 health systems · bars show the reported range
Accuracy = agreement with two clinicians' chart review (higher is better). Agents ran on a local 35-billion-parameter open-weight model (inside the health system). “Single AI call” (in the study: a single LLM, or large language model, call) is the chatbot in our race: one question, one answer.
The chatbot answers.
The agent investigates.
Fastest is not the same as right. The difference was not a smarter model: it went and looked (tools), and it followed a protocol written by clinicians (a skill). And two clinicians still checked the answers.
